What we receive.

This site requests no personal data and has no forms or mailing list. We hear from you only by call or email, and only what you choose to say, for instance an address, a number to ring back and a note about the trouble.

How it is used.

Details you give are used for booking, doing the work, following up and answering questions. Nothing is sold.

Cookies and third parties.

The pages themselves set no cookies. The embedded map and fonts load from outside providers, who may log technical details like your IP address, according to their own rules.
