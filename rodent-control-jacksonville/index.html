Before you call.

Read your ZIP code off a bill or letter, since it is the first thing we check. Have a sentence ready on what you saw, heard or smelled and the room involved.

Meanwhile, at home.

  • Tonight, pour pet kibble and birdseed into tubs with locking lids.
  • Empty the pet water bowl before bed and wipe the counters.
  • Hold back on your own traps, because strays blur the trails we have to read.
  • Work out which day the first sound, smudge or odor appeared.

Dust control comes first.

Once rodent waste dries it flakes into fine grit, which is why careful cleaning is wet and unhurried. The advice below mirrors public health guidance, whether or not you ever hire us.

  • Brushing, blowing or vacuuming sends fine particles toward your face.
  • Where a room has been shut, prop the doors and windows open and wait half an hour outside.
  • Slip on disposable gloves, rubber or vinyl, ahead of touching soiled material, and rinse your hands after.
  • After the wait, lift the soaked mess in a single sweep with a paper towel.
  • Pets stay clear of the tied bag until it is inside the outdoor bin.
  • Hand washing ends the job, gloved or otherwise.

Do you offer emergency rodent control in Jacksonville at night?

Callers reach a person at any hour, and same-day visits depend on open slots. Codes closest to Neptune Beach are easier to reach quickly than those near the fifteen-mile edge, so ask for the soonest slot and the technician's estimated arrival.

Is Jacksonville the same as the beach cities nearby?

No. Atlantic Beach, Neptune Beach and Jacksonville Beach keep their own city governments and are not part of the City of Jacksonville. They each have separate pages on this site.

How do I know whether my address is inside range?

Do you know which part of the city a ZIP code covers?

We do not claim to. Our coverage file ties codes to Jacksonville but not to named areas, so we ask for your street instead.
