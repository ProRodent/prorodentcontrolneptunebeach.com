The inland part of the Ponte Vedra area.

Ponte Vedra is an unincorporated area in St. Johns County, west of the Intracoastal Waterway. Its encyclopedia page says the area was long treated as part of Ponte Vedra Beach, and later development, including much of the Nocatee community, led to a separate designation. The ZIP code 32081 was assigned in June 2006 to the area south and southwest of 32082. For a rodent call, give us the home address and the ZIP printed on your mail, and the crew plans the house visit from there.

We use that history only to explain why two similar names exist. For a house with droppings in the attic, the practical question is which ZIP code appears on your mail, and the answer decides whether your page is this one or the Ponte Vedra Beach page.

Is Ponte Vedra a city?

How is Ponte Vedra different from Ponte Vedra Beach?
