Putting the stages in order.

One sequence fits most houses. It opens with the survey and quote, which names the animal. Next come the devices that clear whatever is inside, then sealing to close the doors on newcomers. Cleanup or repair finishes the job when the house needs it.

Nobody has to buy the whole sequence. If a new vent cover or a located smell is all you want, say that, and the quote shows just that item.
