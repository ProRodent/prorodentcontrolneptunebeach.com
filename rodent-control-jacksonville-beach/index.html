Where Jacksonville Beach fits.

Jacksonville Beach is an incorporated coastal city in Duval County. It began as Pablo Beach in 1907 and took the Jacksonville Beach name in 1925. It is not part of the City of Jacksonville, even though its residents vote in that city's mayoral election. For a rodent call, that only matters when you give us the home address.

None of that history alters what a technician checks on a rodent job. The visit still starts with the roofline, the vents, the garage seal and the pipes where they enter the walls, wherever the property sits on the map.

Condos, shops and shared walls.

Buildings with shared walls, attics or service alleys can pass animals from one unit to the next. If you live or work in one, say who owns the common areas. A fix on your side may not hold unless the neighbor's side is looked at, and the manager may need to approve sealing.
