A community without a city hall.

Ponte Vedra Beach is an unincorporated community and suburb in St. Johns County, east of the Intracoastal Waterway and south of the Duval County line. Because it is unincorporated, rules that affect a property, such as permits and zoning, come from the county or from a community association. We make no claim about which applies to your address, so tell us about the house, any rats or mice you have heard and the street address, and we will take it from there.

That matters for a rodent job in one practical way. If sealing involves exterior changes, a homeowners association may need to approve them. Mention an association when you phone, and the technician arranges the job so approval is sought first.

Dust control comes first.

Dust from dried waste may carry hantavirus and other germs, so the routine below steers clear of anything that kicks it into the air.

  • Brushing, blowing or vacuuming sends fine particles toward your face.
  • Air trapped in attics, garages and sheds needs a cross-breeze before anyone enters.
  • Keep a spare pair nearby so no pellet ever meets bare skin.
  • After the wait, lift the soaked mess in a single sweep with a paper towel.
  • Gather towels, gloves and waste in one bag, place it inside a second, and only then carry it out.
  • Work the lather under your nails and up the wrists, then dry on a clean towel.
