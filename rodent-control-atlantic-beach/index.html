A small city with its own government.

Atlantic Beach became a town in 1926 and received its charter in 1929. It later annexed the Seminole Beach area in 1987. Its encyclopedia page describes it as the second largest of the Jacksonville Beaches communities, with a large share of its area made up of water. Damp ground and tidal edges are a reason to ask about entry points at the house.

That background says nothing about rodents. For your house, the useful steps are practical: check the roofline and vents, look at garage seals and note where plants touch the walls. A call describing what you found is what starts a visit.

Handling mouse and rat mess: five precautions.

Sequence counts: ventilate, soak, wipe, bag, wash. Every line here lines up with the federal pages listed after this box.

  • Reach for the spray bottle before the dustpan; nothing is touched until it is thoroughly damp.
  • Where a room has been shut, prop the doors and windows open and wait half an hour outside.
  • Slip on disposable gloves, rubber or vinyl, ahead of touching soiled material, and rinse your hands after.
  • After the wait, lift the soaked mess in a single sweep with a paper towel.
  • Pets stay clear of the tied bag until it is inside the outdoor bin.
  • Work the lather under your nails and up the wrists, then dry on a clean towel.
