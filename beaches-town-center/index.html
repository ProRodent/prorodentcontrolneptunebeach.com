Food service and the back door.

Any busy food business may deal with grease, wet floors, boxes and a steady stream of bins. We make no claim about how any place near this name runs its kitchen. The general lesson is that rodents notice patterns: deliveries at a set hour, trash left out until pickup and a door propped open for air.

  • Keep dumpster lids shut and the pad swept.
  • Store dry goods in lidded bins, off the floor.
  • Fit a brush sweep or door seal on the rear entrance.
  • Cap or screen floor drains that are not in use.
  • Look behind equipment for gaps around pipes and conduit.

None of these is a fault in the building. They are habits and fixtures worth checking at close of business.

A damp-wipe routine for droppings and nests.

None of this is medical advice. Think of it as a brief list of habits that public health pages recommend when rodents have been in a home.

  • A single dry dropping can crumble and release a cloud nobody notices.
